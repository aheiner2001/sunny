Training Gaps & Dual Sign-Off Logic

Welcome to Lesson 4! We now build your second manager endpoint: GET /training/gaps.

This endpoint answers the critical manager question: "Who still needs to finish or redo assigned training?"

1. How Sunny's Dual Sign-Off Works

In Sunny Fleet, training is not a self-administered checklist. It requires a dual handshake between an experienced trainer and the employee:

  1. Step 1: Trainer Sign-off: The designated trainer works with the employee on the van or in the shop, then marks the item complete. The status transitions to awaiting_trainee.
  2. Step 2: Trainee Certification: The employee logs into Sunny and certifies that they were trained and understand the procedure. The status transitions to completed.
  3. Step 3: Retraining Trigger: If an employee repeatedly misses questions during daily vehicle inspections (or a manager flags an incident), the status changes to needs_retrain.

2. Which Statuses Belong in the Manager Report?

Status Included in /training/gaps? Manager Meaning
assigned YES Incomplete: In-progress or waiting on trainer demonstration.
awaiting_trainee YES Incomplete: Trainer signed off; waiting for employee to certify in-app.
overdue YES Incomplete: Deadline passed without dual sign-off.
needs_retrain YES Redo: Excessive inspection misses or manager required retraining.
completed NO (Excluded) Compliant: Both trainer and trainee have signed off.

3. The Fixtures (1 Completed, 1 Incomplete, 1 Retrain)

We test with three clear assignments:

  • Assignment A (John Smith - Pressure Washer): Status completed → Excluded.
  • Assignment B (Alex Rivera - Chemical Dilution): Status awaiting_trainee → Included (Incomplete).
  • Assignment C (Sarah Connor - Ladder Safety): Status needs_retrain → Included (Needs Redo).
