Do You Need to Add a Stage?

The short answer is NO—AWS gives you $default for free!

Short Answer: NO!

You do not need to create or add a new stage.

AWS automatically creates a stage called $default with Auto-deploy: ON.

What is a Stage anyway?

A "Stage" in AWS is like an environment tag (e.g. dev, staging, or prod).

  • In old AWS REST APIs, you had to manually create a stage called prod or v1.
  • In modern HTTP APIs, AWS simplified this: they give you $default.

Because $default has Auto-deploy enabled, every change you make to routes or integrations goes live instantly—no manual clicking "Deploy API" needed!

What Should Your URL Look Like?

With the $default stage, your URL doesn't have an awkward /prod or /dev prefix in the path: 

https://abcdef123.execute-api.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/reports/weekly/preview

Clean, simple, and ready to go!

Just click Next past the Stage step and click Create!
